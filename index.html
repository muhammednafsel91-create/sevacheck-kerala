      ℹ️ Important Notes

      How SevaCheck Kerala Works

      Applying for government services does not need to involve multiple trips to offices just to find out which document is missing.

      1. Search your service

      Look up the certificate, licence, or document you need through our quick search index.

      2. Check basic eligibility

      Review the standard criteria required under Kerala state guidelines or central requirements.

      3. Prepare your documents

      Use our interactive checklists to tick off documents before you visit an Akshaya centre or submit online.

      4. Visit the official portal or service centre

      Access genuine, official government portals (such as e-District Kerala, Parivahan, or Passport Seva) without falling for phishing or third-party agent portals.

      5. Apply and track your application

      Submit through official channels and save your application receipt or acknowledgement number.

      About SevaCheck Kerala

      SevaCheck Kerala is an independent information and guidance platform created to reduce confusion regarding public administrative procedures in Kerala.

      Our Core Rules

      • No Personal Identifiers Collected: We do not ask for, process, or store your Aadhaar digits, passwords, or personal credentials.
      • No Government Affiliation: We are not a government agency, not an Akshaya centre, and not affiliated with the Government of Kerala.
      • Official Redirects Only: Whenever we link out, we only link to verified government domains (`.gov.in`, `.nic.in`).
      • No Guaranteed Claims: We never promise fast approvals or document issuance, as only authorized government officers hold that statutory power.

      Feedback & Updates

      Rules and required proofs change periodically through official gazette notifications. If you notice outdated information, verify with the official department website.
