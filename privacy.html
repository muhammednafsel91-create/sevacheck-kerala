Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. About SevaCheck Kerala

SevaCheck Kerala is an independent public-service information and navigation website that helps users understand government and public services, including eligibility, required documents, application steps, and official links.

SevaCheck Kerala is not a government website and is not affiliated with or operated by any government department.

2. Information We Collect

SevaCheck Kerala does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as Aadhaar numbers, OTPs, passwords, bank or payment details, government account login credentials, or identity documents.

The website does not currently provide user accounts, login systems, contact forms, feedback forms, rating systems, file uploads, or other features that directly collect personal information.

Users may voluntarily contact SevaCheck Kerala through the official contact email address provided on the website. Any information included in an email is voluntarily provided by the user.

Search queries entered on SevaCheck Kerala are processed locally in the user's browser and are not sent to a SevaCheck server or external search service.

The website uses browser local storage only to remember the user's light or dark theme preference. This does not contain personal information.

3. How We Use Information

Information voluntarily provided by users, such as information included in emails, may be used to respond to enquiries, receive feedback, improve the website, maintain website security, and improve the accuracy and usability of the information provided.

SevaCheck Kerala does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

SevaCheck Kerala may use third-party services, including advertising services such as Google AdSense.

When such services are enabled, third-party providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to serve advertisements, measure advertising performance, maintain security, or provide related services.

Google and other third-party advertising providers may use information about users' visits to this and other websites to provide relevant advertisements. Users may have options to manage or opt out of personalized advertising through controls provided by Google or other applicable third-party services.

SevaCheck Kerala does not sell users' personal information to advertising providers.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The SevaCheck Kerala website itself does not currently use cookies for tracking purposes.

However, third-party services, including advertising services such as Google AdSense, may use cookies or similar technologies when enabled on the website.

Users can manage or disable cookies through their browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality or third-party services.

6. Analytics and Tracking

SevaCheck Kerala does not currently use Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, Microsoft Clarity, Hotjar, or similar third-party analytics or tracking services.

If analytics or other tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

7. Website Security

SevaCheck Kerala takes reasonable measures to protect information voluntarily provided by users against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no website or online service can guarantee complete security of information transmitted or stored online.

8. Data Retention

Information voluntarily provided by users is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, such as responding to enquiries, handling feedback, maintaining records, or addressing security and legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or securely disposed of, subject to applicable legal requirements.

9. User Privacy Choices

Users may contact SevaCheck Kerala to ask questions about personal information they have voluntarily provided, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion where reasonably possible and where no legal or legitimate reason requires the information to be retained.

Users may also manage certain cookie and advertising preferences through their browser settings or through controls provided by third-party services such as Google.

10. Government and External Links

SevaCheck Kerala provides links to official government websites and other external websites where appropriate.

When a user follows an external link, they leave SevaCheck Kerala and become subject to the privacy policy and terms of the respective external website.

SevaCheck Kerala does not control how external websites collect, use, or process information.

11. Hosting and Technical Information

SevaCheck Kerala is hosted as a static website through GitHub Pages.

Although the SevaCheck Kerala application itself does not collect or transmit personal information to a custom server, the hosting infrastructure may process technical information associated with requests to the website, such as IP addresses, browser information, timestamps, and other technical request data, for purposes such as security, abuse prevention, and service operation.

Such processing is subject to the applicable policies of the hosting provider.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

SevaCheck Kerala may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the website, services, technologies, legal requirements, advertising services, or data-handling practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page along with the date of the latest update.
